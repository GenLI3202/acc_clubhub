Today’s 100k ride to Peißenberg was all about strong legs, great views, and even better company. We rolled through beautiful roads, climbed together, pushed when it mattered, and enjoyed every part of the route as a team.

What made this ride special was not just the distance, and not only the scenery, but the feeling of riding it together. Some riders set the tempo, some held the pace steady, and some dug deep on the climbs to stay with the group. It was hard in the right way — and that is exactly what made it fun. This is the kind of ride we love: effort, scenery, and the shared satisfaction of finishing a proper route together.
