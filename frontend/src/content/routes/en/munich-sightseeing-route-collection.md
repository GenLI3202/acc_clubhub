This post is not meant to represent one locked final navigation line. It is a route-library collection built around the Komoot Munich cycling guide, meant to serve as a broader entry point for sightseeing-oriented rides in and around Munich.

## What this post is for

It works best as a discovery post on the routes page: a place to start if you want a city ride, an Isar-side spin, or a relaxed suburban loop without choosing one exact route upfront.

## Why “sightseeing route collection” fits

- The guide covers classic Munich and near-suburban riding ideas rather than one race-style line
- The name is easier to understand for new members browsing the routes page
- It leaves room for multiple future route spin-offs
- It reads like stable library content instead of a temporary event draft

## Surface and ride feel

Expect a mixed route environment with a majority of rideable paved sections plus selected compact gravel, riverside, or park-path sections. In club terms, it leans more toward sightseeing and relaxed weekend riding than technical gravel.
