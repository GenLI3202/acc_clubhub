Join us for a relaxed ride through München Nord after work this Thursday.

This is an **after-work social ride** for riders who want to get some fresh air, spin their legs after work, and enjoy an easy evening ride with ACC. The pace is friendly, but participants should be comfortable with basic road riding.

We will meet at **Pelkovenstraße 143-147, 80992 München**. The route is about **64.1 km** with around **220 m** of elevation gain. Estimated moving time is **2h 41m**. Overall, this München Nord route is designed as a medium-distance social ride for a weekday evening.

### About the ride

This is not designed as a hard training ride. The idea is to enjoy an easy **after-work ride**, relax a bit after the workday, and ride together with other ACC cyclists.

If you want to finish the day with a slightly longer but still friendly evening route, this one should be a good fit.

All cycling enthusiasts are welcome. If you are comfortable with the route length and expected ride rhythm, feel free to join.

---

### Logistics

As this is a weekday evening ride, the total duration will be a bit longer than a shorter after-work loop.  
Please make sure you have enough time for the full evening and bring basic nutrition and lights if needed.
