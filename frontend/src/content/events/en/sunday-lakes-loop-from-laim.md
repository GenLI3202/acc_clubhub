This Sunday we are riding out from **the southwest exit area of Laim S-Bahn station** and heading toward the lakes for a relaxed social ride. The route is about **57.2 km** and the estimated riding time is **3 h 21 min**, so it is a solid weekend ride without being overly hard.

The first part rolls out of the city before opening into quieter roads, with forest sections and lakeside stretches later on. It is not meant to be a smash-fest training ride, but more the kind of route that lets you enjoy the day while still getting in a proper ride.
