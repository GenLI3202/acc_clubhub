We will adapt the water stop and regrouping to the situation on the day, so the actual finish time may be more flexible than the Komoot riding time.

### Ride Rhythm and Water Stop

The route focuses on an easy ride around the English Garden, with the summer atmosphere and a relaxed stop along the way as the main idea. We will keep the pace accessible, regroup as needed, and avoid any speed pressure.

The water stop is optional and safety comes first. Please check your own condition and the local situation before entering the water. If you do not want to swim, you can simply rest, chat, or take photos on the side.

We'll flag safety reminders and look out for each other, but water activities carry some inherent risk. Please be realistic about your own ability and stay responsible for your own safety and belongings — for anything that happens during the water stop, participants take responsibility for themselves.

---

### Return / Logistics

This is a short city ride, so there is no separate train return plan. The final point will depend on the actual water stop and group rhythm on the day. Riders can return independently from the English Garden area or towards Münchner Freiheit.
