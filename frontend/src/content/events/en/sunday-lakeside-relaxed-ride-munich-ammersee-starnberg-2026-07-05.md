Join ACC this Sunday for a relaxed lakeside ride.

We will start in Munich and ride toward **Ammersee**, with lunch planned at **Klostergasthof Andechs**. After lunch, riders can continue the full route back toward Munich, or return independently by **S-Bahn S8** from **Herrsching** or **Starnberg**.

The ride is social and relaxed in spirit: long enough for a proper Sunday outing, but not designed as a drop ride.
