# Friday Social Ride

## About

Weekly Friday evening social ride! Suitable for all levels, enjoy Munich's summer evenings together.

## Details

- **Time**: Every Friday 18:30
- **Meeting Point**: English Garden South Gate
- **Distance**: ~25-30 km
- **Pace**: Easy 22-25 km/h
- **Level**: Beginner-friendly

## Route Highlights

- Isar River path
- English Garden loop
- Olympic Park
- Flat route, no climbing

## What to Bring

- Your bike + helmet
- Bike lights recommended (for after dark)
- Coffee break included

**No registration needed - just show up!**

But if you want weekly notifications, subscribe below.
