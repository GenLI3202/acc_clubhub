# 2025 Summer Alps Cycling Tour

Join us for a 3-day Alpine cycling adventure!

## Details

- **Date**: July 15-17, 2025
- **Meeting Point**: Munich Central Station
- **Level**: Intermediate to Advanced
- **Total Distance**: ~280 km
- **Total Elevation**: ~4500 m

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Munich → Garmisch

- Distance: 90km
- Elevation: 1200m

### Day 2: Zugspitze Loop

- Distance: 100km
- Elevation: 1800m

### Day 3: Return to Munich

- Distance: 90km
- Elevation: 1500m

## Included

- 2 nights accommodation
- Breakfast + Dinner
- Support stations
- Guide service

## Requirements

- Good fitness level
- Capable of 100km+ rides
- Road or gravel bike required

**Limited spots - first come, first served!**
