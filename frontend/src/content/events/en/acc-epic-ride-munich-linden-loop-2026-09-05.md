This ACC Epic Ride starts from the south side of Munich, rolls through **Linden** and **Schäftlarn**, and returns to Munich as a road loop. The route is around **76.4 km** with roughly **564 m** of climbing, making it a moderate road ride.

This is not a beginner spin. Please register only if you are comfortable riding more than 70 km on the road, can manage your own pacing, and come prepared with food, drink, and basic repair items.

### Ride Notes

- This is a road loop starting and finishing in Munich.
- The route passes **Linden** and **Schäftlarn**, with distance and elevation suited to a moderate weekend training ride.
- Please check your bike in advance, especially tires, brakes, and drivetrain.
- Bring enough water and nutrition instead of relying only on possible stops along the route.

### Registration / Join

**Please register with your real name through the official event page.**

The ride is limited to **15 riders**. Only a completed real-name RSVP through the website counts as registration.

Real-name registration is important for proper participation records and insurance coverage.

### Insurance and Fee

ACC provides event insurance for **non-members** who complete registration through the official event page.

Participation fee for non-members: **€2 for non-students / €1 for students, including PhD students**. **ACC members join for free**. Students should show a valid student ID, and the fee is collected at the meeting point.
