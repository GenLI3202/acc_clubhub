Join us this Saturday for a relaxed countryside loop southwest of Munich.

The route starts in Munich and heads out along quieter country roads, passing forests, meadows, and Bavarian villages. The profile is mostly rolling, without long or especially steep climbs, making it a good fit for a weekend coffee ride.

We will plan one coffee stop along the way for refuelling, chatting, and photos before completing the loop back to Munich. The spirit is social and friendly: enjoy the scenery and the ride, not chase speed.

### Ride Notes

- Around **89 km / 715 m**, suitable for a medium-long weekend endurance ride.

- After the coffee stop, the route loops back to Munich without requiring public transport.
